# Points

#### What are Velox points?

Our way of recognizing those who contribute to Velox’s success. They reflect a user’s overall engagement on our exchange.

1m points are distributed each week.

#### How to earn points

* Perps traders share 750,000 points each week (determined by your volume, OI, & other factors)
* Vault depositors share 250,000 points each week (proportional to the value and length of time of your deposit)
* Referrers receive 1 point for every 5 points earned by their direct referrals, and 1 point for every 20 points earned by their indirect referrals (not part of the same 1m weekly points distribution for traders and vault depositors)

#### Boosters

Anyone who owns 1 or more Galxe OATs from Velox's testnet quests is eligible for a temporary 4-week points boost. To activate the boost, simply link the EVM wallet(s) containing the OAT(s) to your Velox account. Each unique OAT grants a 100% points boost, up to a maximum of 400%. 

#### Boxes

Users can open loot boxes by moving volume (taker or maker)

The more volume required to obtain the box, the greater chance of a more valuable reward

| Box Type | How to Obtain       |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| Bronze   | Every $25k volume   |
| Silver   | Every $100k volume  |
| Gold     | Every $250k volume  |
| Crystal  | Every $500k volume  |

#### NFTs

There are 6 rarities of Velox NFTs:

* Common
* Uncommon
* Rare
* Epic
* Legendary
* Mythic

Each loot box is guaranteed to contain 1 NFT of random rarity

Each NFT has a hidden points value that will be revealed once the points program concludes

#### Leagues

All users are grouped into leagues based on their total points holdings. Players move up and down leagues at the start of each new week when points are distributed. There is no reward for climbing leagues, it’s simply for fun!


Wood - bottom 30% of points holders

Iron - next 25%

Gold - next 18%

Platinum - next 12%

Diamond - next 8%

Master - next 5%

Grandmaster - top 2%
